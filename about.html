About Me

A little bit about who I am, where I came from, and what I'm up to.

Hey, I'm Danny 👋

I'm a Computer Science graduate from Occidental College, where I also minored in Mathematics. My interest in tech started in a pretty unexpected way: after my mom accidentally cut the charger for our family laptop, I found myself tinkering with wires just to get back to playing League of Legends. That curiosity about how things work stuck with me and grew into a genuine passion for building software.

At Occidental, I got to explore a wide range of CS topics — algorithms, data structures, software development, and mobile apps — while my math minor sharpened the analytical side of my thinking. Outside of coursework, I participated in a Research Experience for Undergraduates (REU) at Boise State University, where I researched adversarial attacks on AI-based malware detectors and presented my findings at the Idaho Conference on Undergraduate Research.

I'm interested in roles across software engineering, cybersecurity, and project management — basically anywhere I can keep building and problem-solving. When I'm not at a computer, you'll find me at the gym, hiking, or swimming. (Skydiving is next on the list.)
